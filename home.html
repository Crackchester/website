Cyber Security Society at the University of Manchester


About Us

We are Hackchester, the University of Manchester’s Cyber Security Society! We welcome students from all courses and degree levels who are passionate about protecting the digital world and furthering their knowledge of cyber. No prior knowledge is required to join, only a positive attitude and willingness to learn! Our society provides a platform for like-minded individuals to explore, master new skills and collaborate on projects.

If you have any questions, queries, or ideas for specific sessions, please reach out to us either in person or through our socials!

Events

We have weekly meetings every Wednesday from 5-6pm in the Kilburn building, room G41. However, some sessions run 4-6 so please check our socials regularly for updates!

Please follow us (see below) for an insight into our upcoming sessions. Anyone can join us for any session but do check if the event has any prerequisites (e.g. some of our sessions require a Linux laptop/virtual machine).

Socials

Join our Discord server for useful resources, opportunities, and to learn about upcoming events. Also, follow us on our other socials to keep in the loop with all Hackchester activities!
